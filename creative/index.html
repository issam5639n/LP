The Creative Conversion Roadmap

Stop making ads. Start building ads that sell.

Most ecommerce brands don't have a "bad creative problem." They have a strategy problem. The creative is only as strong as the message behind it.

01
Foundation

Start With The Persona

Before thinking about the design, hook or video, understand exactly who you're speaking to.

Don't say:

"Our skincare product is perfect for everyone."


Say:

"For women in their 20s and 30s who are tired of buying products that promise clearer skin but never deliver."

02
Message

Find The Right Offer

Your creative can get attention and still fail to convert if the offer doesn't make the decision easy.

Weak offer:

"Buy our product today."


Stronger offer:

" Get 2 months of our best-selling formula + free delivery when you order today."

03
Angle

Choose The Right Angle

The same product can be sold from multiple psychological angles. Your job is to discover which angle resonates with the market.

Example angles:

Problem → Solution
Before → After
Founder Story
Social Proof
Us vs Them
Product Education
Censored / Pattern Interrupt

04
Hook

Win The First 3 Seconds

Nobody cares about your logo. Nobody cares about your product yet. Your first job is to make them stop scrolling.

Hook formula:

Pattern interrupt + curiosity + callout specific problem + callout persona


"Your ads aren't failing because your targeting is bad..."

05
Value

Make The Viewer Understand The Result

Don't just explain what your product is. Show the person why the product matters to them.

Feature:

"Our formula contains X ingredient."


Benefit:

"That ingredient helps support X result, so your customer can achieve Y without Z."

06
CTA

Tell Them What To Do Next

Don't end the creative with a vague "Shop Now." Give the viewer a clear reason to take action.

Better CTA:

"Tap below to get your first order with free delivery."


The CTA should connect directly to the offer you introduced in the creative (and Landing Page).

Testing Framework

Don't test random creatives. Test variables. One at a time.

Your goal isn't to find one "winning ad." Your goal is to discover which combination of persona + offer + angle produces the strongest response.

VARIABLE 01

Persona

Who is most likely to buy? Test different customer motivations, problems and situations.

VARIABLE 02

Offer

Test different bundles, discounts, guarantees, bonuses and reasons to buy now.

VARIABLE 03

Angle

Test different ways of framing the same product: problem-solution, transformation, story, education and comparison.

The Simple Formula

A converting creative isn't complicated.

When the strategy is right, the creative becomes much easier to produce and scale.

Persona + Offer + Angle + Hook + Value + CTA
