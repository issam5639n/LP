Is this right for you?

A good partnership starts
with a good fit.

The strongest starting point is a product people already buy and a founder ready to invest in a process.

We could be a strong fit if…

  • You sell physical products through an eCommerce brand.
  • You have real customers and existing proof of demand.
  • You’re around $3k–$10k a month, or higher and stuck.
  • You can allocate at least $900 a month to Meta ad spend.
  • You want to understand the strategy behind your growth.

This approach may not fit if…

  • Your product hasn’t been validated with real customers.
  • You need guaranteed overnight results.
  • You can’t currently invest in testing and ad spend.
  • You sell services, coaching or agency offers.
  • You’re dropshipping into the US/EU without an established brand.

Ask me directly on Instagram
What exactly do you do for my brand?

I manage Meta ads, create direct-response image ads and build the strategy behind them. The work also includes offer creation and product page optimization, so the message stays consistent from the ad to the checkout.

How much ad budget do I need?

You should be able to allocate at least $900 per month to Meta ad spend. We’ll look at your margins, average order value and goals to decide what budget makes sense. Ad spend is separate from the fee for working together.

What does it cost to work together?

The fee depends on the scope your brand needs. The value figures above are my estimates of the individual services, not the price you will pay. We’ll discuss the scope and fee on the call before you decide.

How quickly can I expect results?

First we assess the account, the offer and the numbers. Then we test creative and scale the concepts that earn more budget. The featured skincare case took 278 days. Your timeline will depend on demand, margins, budget and execution.

How does the guarantee work?

If, in the first 7 days, you feel my plan won’t benefit you, I’ll return your money, no questions asked. After 60 days, if you haven’t seen results that satisfy you, we’ll work for FREE until you’re 100% satisfied.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

No long-term contract is required. We’ll review the scope, working arrangement and terms before you decide to move forward.

What happens after I apply?

I review your answers to understand your brand, goals and budget. If there’s a fit, we’ll arrange the free strategy call. You can also watch the BEYOND Protocol training or contact me directly on Instagram.
